This module contains PDF slide decks that cover observations from three different types of sensors/parts of the electromagnetic spectrum. 
Passive microwave, synthetic aperture radar and long-wave infrared (used for surface temperature).

There is both an ice concentration activity and a sea surface temperature activity posted, using passive microwave and long-wave infrared satellite data respectively. 

For the SAR data, there are some questions in the slide deck and follow up activities suggested at the end.

For the sea ice concentration and sea surface temperature activities, please let me know if you are interested in these activities. I would be happy to send you the data. 
You can also get the data yourself by generating a login at the various data providers (below)

Sea Ice Concentration Activity:

Passive microwave brightness temperatures are available on g-portal https://gportal.jaxa.jp/gpr/?lang=en
Passive microwave sea ice concentration is available through the Copernicus Climate Data Store https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets

Sea Surface Temperature Activity:

Landsat data is available from USGS EarthExplorer https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/
Sentinel 3 data is available from the Copernicus Dataspace Ecosystem https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/data-collections/copernicus-sentinel-missions/sentinel-3
VIIRS data is available from NASA https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/missions-and-measurements/science-domain/viirs-L0-L1/

SAR data is available through the Alaska SAR Facility Vertex search https://search.asf.alaska.edu

There are other places to get the data. These are the ones I have found most straightforward. You can generally get a user profiles with your academic email.
